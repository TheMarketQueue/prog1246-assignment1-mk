About this page

Why League of Legends?

I really enjoy the game as I have been playing since I was 7. I like looking at the different champion skin designs and their gameplay. Zed comes first on my list as its my best champion, I especially like the Shockblade skin. The lightning and white armour make its splash art stand out and the animations in game are.

Other favourites include Frosted Ezreal, Spirit Blossom Riven and Dark Cosmic Jhin. This page brings my picks together in one place. The list on the side includes the rest of my favourite skins.
